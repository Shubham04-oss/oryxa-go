# Planning a working session

1. Pin down the outcome: what should be decided or exist when the session ends.
2. Find the people with the teams sub-agent, and keep the list to the people
   the outcome needs.
3. Choose the length. Default to 30 minutes unless the outcome needs more.
4. Draft the agenda as a few timed items, each ending in a decision or
   something made.
5. Say what each person should prepare, and by when.
6. Show the plan to the member. Only after they confirm, invite people or book
   time with the toolboxes they have.
7. After the session, offer to write up the decisions and follow-ups.
