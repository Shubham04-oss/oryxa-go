## 5. License: the options, side by side

The decision is the owner's; this only frames it.

| Option | Says | Fits if |
|---|---|---|
| **FSL** (Functional Source License, converts to Apache 2.0 or MIT after two years) | Read it, run it, change it; don't sell a competing product. Opens fully later | You want developer goodwill and a clear "not a competitor" line |
| **BUSL 1.1** (converts after a set date) | Same idea, with more knobs (use grant, date) | You want to tune exactly what's allowed |
| **All rights reserved, source visible** (a short LICENSE saying so) | Look, don't use | You want the most room to change course |

HN's first question is "what's the license", so whichever you pick, say why
in one line in the README.
