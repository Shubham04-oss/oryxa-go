# Platforms: one page, one routine each

*2026-10-15. A routine is something done without deciding to. Each page
here gives one platform its own: what it is for, who is there, the one
format that works on it, the routine by day and week, the rules, the first
ten moves, and what we count. The calendar that joins them is
[../calendar.md](../calendar.md).*

## The voice, in three rules

1. **Say it the way you'd say it out loud.** The product's own rule
   ([direction.md](../../design/direction.md)) applies to posts. No
   internal words ([positioning.md](../positioning.md#words)), no "🚀", no
   "excited to announce".
2. **Show the work.** A real screen, a real commit, a real number, or
   nothing. No example data, no mockups passed off as the product, no
   numbers we haven't measured.
3. **Never ask for the metric.** No "please upvote", "star us", "like and
   share". On Hacker News and Product Hunt it is a rule; everywhere else it
   is the difference between a builder and a marketer.

## One recording, many cuts

The Friday recording is the week's content. One two-minute recording
becomes: the X clip (Wednesday), the LinkedIn demo post (Friday), a YouTube
short (Wednesday) and a chapter of the monthly long video, the changelog's
picture, and the release note's "see it" link. Nothing is recorded twice.

## Founder or agent

| Only the founder | The agents |
|---|---|
| Posting, replying, DMs, calls, anything with a name on it | Drafting from [content-bank.md](../content-bank.md) and from merged PRs |
| Launch days | The mentions digest: GitHub, X, email, Reddit, HN, each morning |
| The ship log's final words | The pipeline sheet, the metrics query, the partner digest |
| Decisions on the partner page, pricing, the license | Captions, cuts, the changelog page, labels on issues |

The three agents on the board can take these as a docs lane
(`docs/gtm/**`, the changelog source, the metrics query), one at a time.

## What we count

One sheet, one row a week, one column per platform: followers, posts,
replies received, link clicks where the platform shows them, sign-ups with
that platform as the referrer (ask on sign-up: "Where did you hear about
Oryxa?", one optional field). The number that matters is at the end of the
row: sign-ups and installs, not followers. [../README.md](../README.md#the-numbers).

## What we never do

- Buy followers, votes, stars or comments. Trade upvotes. Join "engagement
  pods".
- Post example data, mockups or a roadmap as if shipped.
- Announce a feature the dry-run hasn't passed.
- Argue with a critic in public for more than two replies. Thank them, fix
  it, post the fix.
- DM anyone who hasn't shown the problem (a post, a question, a job ad).
- Schedule a post the founder hasn't read.
