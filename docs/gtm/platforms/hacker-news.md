# Hacker News

## Why here

One prepared Show HN can bring a thousand serious readers in a day, and
they will read [north-star.md](../../north-star.md) and
[slack-consent-loop.md](../../design/slack-consent-loop.md) and say what is
wrong with them. That is worth more than the traffic. Between launches, it
is where the technical series gets tested and where useful comments earn
the account its standing.

## Who is there

Engineers, CTOs, founders. Some of them run Slack teams of 20–150 people
and will install it with a teammate that afternoon. Many will never be
customers and will still tell their friends.

## The thing

**One Show HN**, after the dry-run passes and a stranger can sign up and run
an ask without us. Before it, the technical posts go up as plain links the
day they're published. Always, two useful comments a week on threads about
agents, permissions, Slack bots, Durable Objects, Go.

## The Show HN

| | |
|---|---|
| **When** | Week 12: Tuesday Jan 5 or Wednesday Jan 6, 2027, 18:30 IST (08:00 US East). Not a holiday week, not a Monday, not a Friday |
| **Title** (under 80 characters) | "Show HN: Oryxa – a team of people and their agents, with consent between agents" · alt: "Show HN: Oryxa – every person gets an agent; agents ask each other with one click (Go)" |
| **Link** | The website, where the demo and the sign-up are. The repo and the design docs go in the first comment. (Show HN rules: something people can try) |
| **First comment** | Who I am. The problem in two lines: AI is single-player, work isn't. What it is in three: a team of people and their agents; each person's agent is theirs; agents ask each other with one click of consent, and Slack stays. The sixty seconds. How it's built, three bullets with file links (agent identity and isolation, the consent row lock, the guard). What's not built, honestly (parleys in production, remembered consent, N-of-M, Teams, Google verification in progress). What I want feedback on. The ask: "try it with one teammate; the first ask takes twenty minutes". Under 300 words |
| **The day** | No calls, no other posts. Reply to every comment within fifteen minutes for twelve hours, then every hour for a day. Every useful thread becomes an issue, with the comment linked |
| **After** | Two weeks later, a post: "What we fixed after Show HN", with the issues closed |
| **If it doesn't take** (under ten points in two hours) | That's allowed. Don't repost it, don't ask anyone to vote. Resubmit months later only after a substantial change, with a new title |

What partners may do: comment, if they genuinely use it, about what they
used it for. Never upvote on request. We never ask anyone to upvote,
anywhere, and never post the link with "support us". HN detects rings and
the penalty is the whole launch.

## Rules

- The site guidelines are the rules: no vote requests, no editorialised
  titles, no self-promotion in comments beyond the Show HN, no using the
  comments to sell.
- Never mention Oryxa in a comment unless asked or it answers the question
  directly.
- Disagree with a critic once, with facts, then stop. "You're right, fixed"
  is the strongest reply on HN.
- The link must work for a stranger: sign-up, verify email, workspace, Slack
  install, first ask, with no step that needs us.

## What we count

Points and comments (once). Visitors and sign-ups that day and the week
after, by referrer. Issues filed from the thread, and how many closed in
two weeks. Workspaces that installed Slack in the following week.

## Founder or agent

The founder writes every word on HN. Agents search, draft answers, watch and
file.
