# India

## Why here

Home ground. The founder's network is here, calls are in IST, Dodo handles
GST, and Indian startups of 20–200 people run on Slack and Google Workspace
in exactly the shape [design-partners.md](../design-partners.md#who-qualifies)
describes. Thirty of the first hundred names are here. Presence is global;
the first calls are local.

## Who is there

| Where | Who | What fits |
|---|---|---|
| The founder's own groups (WhatsApp, Slack, alumni) | Peers, ex-colleagues | The first thirty names; the warm-intro template |
| SaaSBoomi (community and events) | SaaS founders and operators | Community answers; a talk; partner introductions |
| Programs: Peak XV Surge and Spark, Antler India, Google for Startups Accelerator India | Founders; the programs themselves | Apply if it fits the owner's plans; introductions regardless. **Verify** each program's current dates |
| Hasgeek (Rootconf, The Fifth Elephant), Gophers India and Bangalore Go meetups | Engineers | A talk: "Twelve services, one binary" or "Consent between agents". Talks become the long video |
| Product communities (The Product Folks and the like) | Product people | The cross-team question; the design-partner post where allowed |
| Inc42, YourStory | Press | A launch story at phase 3: an Indian company, agents for Slack teams everywhere, built with coding agents |
| LinkedIn India | Everyone | Already the main channel; the India list runs first |

## The thing

**Thirty names, one talk, one community, and press at launch.**

## The routine

| When | What | Who |
|---|---|---|
| Week 1 | The thirty names written down, with the reason each fits | Founder |
| Weeks 2–4 | The warm messages, ten a week; calls 10:00–12:00 IST, Tuesday to Thursday | Founder |
| Monthly | One community post or answer (SaaSBoomi); one meetup or talk proposal | Founder |
| Week 6 | A talk proposed (Hasgeek or a Go meetup) for the new year; the talk is the long video's script | Founder, agents draft |
| Week 15 | The press pitch to Inc42 and YourStory, with the demo, the numbers and the "built with three coding agents" story | Founder, agents draft |
| At pricing-on | INR shown where Dodo shows it; GST invoices checked | macbookpro |

## Rules

- DPDP Act: opt-in for every list, unsubscribe honoured the same day.
- Calls in IST mornings for India, IST evenings for the US and EU; never
  both on the same day's morning.
- A program is applied for only if the owner wants what it gives; a program
  is never a channel in itself.
- Press at phase 3, not before: a story about a product strangers can't yet
  sign up for is a story nobody can act on.

## The first ten moves

1. The thirty names.
2. Join SaaSBoomi's community; read for a week.
3. The first ten warm messages.
4. The first five India calls.
5. The talk proposal.
6. The community post, where allowed.
7. Program applications, if any, by week 6.
8. The INR and GST check at pricing-on.
9. The press pitch, week 15.
10. A meetup talk, in the new year, recorded.

## What we count

Partners from India; India calls held; talks given; press published.

## Founder or agent

The founder's network is the founder's. Agents draft the talk, the pitch and
