# YouTube, and video everywhere

## Why here

The demo is the product. A champion shows their boss a video, not a doc. A
short is the X clip and the LinkedIn demo post. The long walkthrough is the
sales call we don't have to take. YouTube hosts it all and ranks the name.

## Who is there

Both audiences: a buyer searching "Oryxa", a developer who wants to see it
before reading, and anyone who followed a link.

## The thing

**Two shorts a week cut from Friday's recording, and one long walkthrough a
month.** Every video shows a real workspace of ours, with two real accounts,
no example data, captions always.

| Video | Length | Script |
|---|---|---|
| The ask | 60 s | [content-bank.md](../content-bank.md#the-ask-sixty-seconds). Captions, no voice |
| Secrets and deny | 90 s | Same page. Captions |

| Set up Oryxa for your team | 8 min | The twenty-minute path at 2× with voice, every trap named |
| How the consent loop works, with the code | 12 min | For developers: the card, the row lock, the lease, the guard, the files on screen |

## The routine

| When | What | Who |
|---|---|---|
| Friday block | Record: the week's clip (under two minutes) and the material for one short. Action in the middle third of the frame so the vertical crop works | Founder |
| Friday to Monday | Two shorts cut, captioned, vertical; the clip captioned for X and LinkedIn | Agents |
| Monday | Review the cuts; fix a caption; approve | Founder |
| Wednesday, Friday | A short published, with the sign-up link and the repo in the description | Founder (scheduled) |
| Monthly, in a quiet week | The long video: scripted by Monday, recorded by Wednesday, published Friday with chapters | Founder, agents script |
| After any UI change | Re-record the clip the change makes wrong. A video of an old screen is a bug | Founder |

## Rules

- Captions on everything. No music under a voice. The first three seconds
  show the ask being typed.
- Titles are the sentence, not the hook: "Ask in Slack; the person who knows
  approves with one click."
- Light mode, 1280×800, a cursor that moves slowly, the browser's own window
  and nothing else.
- Never a partner's workspace. Never a Google "unverified app" screen edited
  out; show it and say why.
- Descriptions carry the sign-up link, the repo, and the chapter list.
  Nothing else.

## The first ten moves

1. The channel: the bio line, the approve card as the banner, the links.
2. The sixty-second ask, as a video and as a short.
3. The ninety-second secrets-and-deny.
4. The two-minute team cut.
5. Week 3: the first weekly short.
6. Week 10–11 (the holiday weeks): "Oryxa in ten minutes".
7. Week 12: "How the consent loop works, with the code", the day after HN.
8. Week 14: "Set up Oryxa for your team".
9. When live: the parley.
10. When public: set up with your coding agent.

## What we count

Views in the first 48 hours; watch-through on the long videos (where people
stop is where the story is unclear); link clicks; sign-ups by referrer.

## Founder or agent

The founder records and speaks. Agents cut, caption, script and schedule.
