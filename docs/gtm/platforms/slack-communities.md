# Slack communities, and our own

## Why here

The champion is already in Slack, and in a Slack community, asking "what do
you use for X" and answering it for others. A good answer there is a warm
introduction. It is also the right place for the partner post, once, where a
community allows it. And it answers the "should we have a Discord" question:
no. A Slack Connect channel per partner is our community.

## Who is there

Communities with the champion in them. Verify every invite link and each
community's rules before joining; some are paid or invite-only.

| Community | Who | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Rands Leadership Slack | Engineering managers and leaders | Large, strict on promotion; answer in the questions channels |
| Mind the Product Slack | Product managers | The cross-team question is theirs too |
| RevOps Co-op | Operations people | Where "who owns this" is a daily question |
| Chief of Staff Network, Operators Guild | Chiefs of staff, operators | Paid or invite-only; join if the founder qualifies |
| SaaSBoomi community | Indian SaaS founders and operators | Home ground; see [india.md](india.md) |

| Cloudflare Developers Discord, Composio's community | Developers on those platforms | The ecosystem angle; [ecosystems.md](ecosystems.md) |
| The founder's own founder groups (WhatsApp, Slack) | Peers | The first thirty names |

## The thing

**Two real answers a week, and the partner post once a month where it is
allowed.** An answer has no link unless asked. A DM goes only to someone
who asked the question we answer.

## Our own community

- **No Discord, no community server** until twenty-five active workspaces.
  An empty server is worse than none.
- **One Slack Connect channel per partner**, `#oryxa-<partner>`, between
  their workspace and ours, answered the same day. That is the support
  channel, the feedback channel and the relationship.
- **Later, `#oryxa-partners`**, shared across partners who opt in, for the
  ship log and the votes on what's next.
- The in-product Requests page and the ship log carry everything else.

## The routine

| When | What | Who |
|---|---|---|
| Weekly | Find the questions: "how do you handle cross-team questions", "Slack bot for internal Q&A", "who owns what", "agent permissions", in each community's search | Agents |
| Tuesday, Thursday | One answer each, from the founder, with a point and no link | Founder |
| Monthly | The partner post ([design-partners.md](../design-partners.md#outreach-templates)) in one community's promotion channel, if it has one. Never in a questions channel | Founder |
| Same day | Every partner's Connect channel answered | Founder |
| Weekly | A digest of community threads that mention the problem, for the LinkedIn list | Agents |

## Rules

- Read the code of conduct and the promotion rules before the first message.
- The founder's real name and company in the profile. No pretending to be a
  user.
- No DM that opens with a pitch. Answer their question in public first.
- If a community says no promotion, there is no partner post there, ever.
- Partners' Connect channels are private; nothing from them is quoted
  without permission.

## The first ten moves

1. Join five: Rands, Mind the Product, RevOps Co-op, Gophers, SaaSBoomi.
2. Read each for a week. Note the channels where the question gets asked.
3. Week 2: the first two answers.
4. Week 3: the Connect channel for the first partner.
5. Week 4: the partner post in the one community that allows it.
6. Week 5: Gophers #showandtell, technical post #2's short form.
7. Week 6: apply to one invite-only community if the founder qualifies.
8. Week 8: the partner post in a second community, if allowed.
9. Week 12: a "we launched on HN, here's what we learned" in the founder
   groups.
10. At twenty-five active workspaces: `#oryxa-partners`.

## What we count

Answers given; DMs received (not sent); partners whose first touch was a
community; the Connect channels' median reply time.

## Founder or agent

Agents find and digest. The founder answers, posts and keeps the Connect
channels.
