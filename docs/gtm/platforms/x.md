# X

## Why here

It is where developers, founders and the developer-relations people at
Cloudflare, Google and Composio watch things get built. One true detail a
day, for sixteen weeks, is how a stranger decides we are real before the
Show HN. It is also where people complain about the problem in public.

## Who is there

Developers and founders; dev-rel; the Go and agents crowd; a few of the
team buyers, but LinkedIn is their home. Mirror the posts to Bluesky and
Threads with a scheduler if it costs nothing extra; no separate routine.

## The thing

**One true detail from the day's work**, in two to four lines, with a real
screen: a commit, a design decision with its reason, a number, a sentence
from a design document. Once a week a clip under twenty seconds. Once a
month a thread. Ten replies a day with a real point. The founder's account
is the voice; the company account reposts it and posts releases.

## The routine

| When | What | Who |
|---|---|---|
| Overnight | Three draft posts from the day's merged PRs, the discussions and [content-bank.md](../content-bank.md), each with the file it comes from | Agents |
| 08:30, 15 min | Pick one, cut it to the bone, post it. Ten replies to accounts in the space (agents, Slack, Go, permissions), each a sentence that adds something | Founder |
| 18:00, 10 min | Answer every reply. One DM at most, to someone who posted about the problem today | Founder |
| Wednesday | The clip: twenty seconds, captions, the ask or whatever shipped | Founder, cut by agents |
| Friday | The ship-log thread: five posts, what shipped this week, with the release link, and one honest "what didn't" | Founder |
| Monthly | One thread condensed from the technical series ([content-bank.md](../content-bank.md#the-technical-series)) | Founder |
| Weekly | Saved searches for the problem ("who owns this", "ask in #help", "access denied" wiki, "chasing for an answer"): a list of ten people who said it this week; the founder replies helpfully to three, DMs at most one | Agents find, founder acts |

## Rules

- No polls for engagement, no "agree?", no quote-dunking, no "🚀", no
  "thrilled". No threads that say nothing until post 7.
- Nothing from a partner's workspace on screen. Our own demo workspace
  only, with our own two accounts.
- A DM goes only to someone who showed the problem, and it answers their
  post before it mentions ours. One per day, at most.
- Two replies to a critic, then thank them and post the fix when it's fixed.
- The company account never posts an opinion. Releases and reposts.
- A post the founder hasn't rewritten in their own words doesn't go out.

## The first ten posts

1. The team in one screen: a teamspace with two people, their agents, and an ask between them. The vision post, once, pinned, ending "Slack stays."
2. First click wins, with the clip of the card turning into "Approved by Raya at 14:02" (#1).
3. One static binary, twelve services, and the route-collision refusal (#13), with the `services` command's output.
4. Three coding agents, one account, `[agent:name]` (#29), with a screenshot of a discussion.
5. Slack's three seconds and what happens before 200 (#15).
6. The words we deleted: the table from direction.md (#33).
7. "A kernel stuck in a cell is killed, not waited on" (#25), the commit.
8. A side conversation: ask a teammate's agent without interrupting them (#43).
9. "An agent in the crow's nest" (#37), the onboarding screen.
10. Ship log #1: the first release.

## What we count

Weekly: followers, impressions on the clip posts, replies received, profile
link clicks, sign-ups that name X. Monthly: how many of the ten people from
the saved searches replied.

## Founder or agent

Agents draft, search, cut clips, digest mentions. The founder posts, replies,
DMs.
