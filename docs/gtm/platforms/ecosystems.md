# Ecosystems: the platforms we are built on

## Why here

Oryxa runs on platforms whose developer teams want showcases: a real
product, in production, with numbers and code. One post from them reaches
more developers than a month of ours, and their startup programs pay for
infrastructure. The offer is always a true technical story; the ask comes
after.

## The thing

**One technical write-up per platform, true and specific, offered to the
people who run their developer community, with one ask after.**

## The routine

| When | What | Who |
|---|---|---|

## Rules

- Give first. The write-up exists before the ask.
- Never say "partnership" in a first message. Say what we built on them and
  what we'd like to show.
- Everything in a write-up is true in production, with numbers from our
  own measurements.
- List prices only; nothing about our own terms with a vendor.
- A platform's program (Launchpad, Activate, Google for Startups) is
  applied for on its own terms; the write-up is separate.
