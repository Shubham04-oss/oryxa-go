# Product Hunt

## Why here

A day of early adopters, a badge for the site, and a page that ranks for the
name. Weaker than it was: third-party guides in 2026 report that the hunter
system no longer gives a day-one boost, that comment quality now weighs
alongside votes, and that the platform removes manufactured launches
([one](https://hub.causo.ai/guides/product-hunt-launch-2026-realistic-playbook),
[two](https://blog.innmind.com/how-to-launch-on-product-hunt-in-2026); no
official announcement found). That suits a comment-first launch from people
who actually used it, which is the only kind we can do.

## When

Phase 3, **Tuesday Feb 2, 2027, 12:31 IST (00:01 US Pacific)**. After the
Show HN, after the Marketplace submission, after pricing is on. Never before
self-serve works for a stranger. Once; not again for six months at least.

## Who is there

Early adopters, indie founders, people who try tools on Tuesdays. Some of
them run small teams on Slack. Most will sign up alone and can't run an ask;
the page must say "bring one teammate".

## The thing

**One day carried by its comments.** A maker's first comment that tells the
story, twenty people who used it saying what for, and the founder answering
everything for twenty-four hours.

## The page

| | |
|---|---|
| Tagline (under 60 characters) | "A team of people and their agents. Slack stays." |
| Description | The story in order ([positioning.md](../positioning.md#the-story-in-order)), 200 words, "bring one teammate" in the last line |
| Gallery | Five images: a teamspace with two people and their agents; the ask in a channel; the owner's DM with the buttons; the answer in the thread; a side conversation with a teammate's agent. Then the sixty-second clip, and the two-minute team cut |
| Maker's first comment | Who I am; the problem; what it does; how it's built in three bullets; what's not built; what partners said (quoted, with permission); the ask: try it with a teammate, tell me what broke |
| Topics | Productivity, Slack, Artificial Intelligence, Developer Tools |
| Pricing | What Dodo shows that day: the team plan, and "free for teams under N" if that is the decision |

## The routine

| When | What | Who |
|---|---|---|
| Week 13 | The page drafted; the maker's comment drafted | Agents draft, founder edits |
| Week 14 | Gallery and clip final. The twenty people asked, personally, one message each: "comment if you used it, about what you used it for; don't upvote if you didn't" | Founder |
| Week 15 | A dry run of the day with the agents: the hour-by-hour, the prepared answers to the twelve objections, the X and LinkedIn posts for the day (one each, no vote request), the website banner, the ship log telling partners | Agents, founder |
| The day | No calls, no other posts. Every comment answered within fifteen minutes for the first twelve hours. The agents watch sign-ups and file every bug | Founder; agents watch |
| The week after | Every useful comment an issue; a "what we fixed" post; the badge on the site if it earned one | Founder, agents |

## Rules

- No asking for upvotes, anywhere, by anyone. No vote exchanges, no
  "support" groups, no hunters for hire.
- Nothing on the page the dry-run hasn't passed.
- Partners comment only if they want to and only about what they used.
- One launch. If it falls flat, it falls flat; the routine on the other
  platforms is what compounds.

## The first ten moves

1. The founder's maker profile, week 10 (an account that is new on the day
   reads as manufactured).
2. Read the current launch guide on Product Hunt itself, week 13.
3. The page draft.
4. The maker's comment draft, read cold by two people.
5. The gallery.
6. The twenty people, asked.
7. The day's timeline and the prepared answers.
8. The posts for X and LinkedIn, the banner, the ship log.
9. The day.
10. The "what we fixed" post.

## What we count

Comments (the number that matters there now), sign-ups that day and the
week after, workspaces that installed Slack within a week, paying teams
within a month.

## Founder or agent

Agents draft, prepare and watch. The founder asks the twenty, posts, and
answers every comment.
