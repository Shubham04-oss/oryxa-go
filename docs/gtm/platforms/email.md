# Email

## Why here

It is the only channel we own. Partners and sign-ups said yes to it. The
ship log is how a partner who missed three calls still knows what shipped,
and cold email is the second outbound channel after LinkedIn, for the
champion who isn't on LinkedIn every day.

## Who is there

Partners, sign-ups who ticked the box, prospects from the list, and, when
pricing is on, billing contacts.

## The thing

**The ship log every second Friday, and three cold touches a day from the
list.** The ship log is under 200 words, plain text, from the founder's
address, reply-to the founder: three things shipped (from the release),
one thing a partner asked for that is in, one question, one number.

## The routine

| When | What | Who |
|---|---|---|
| Thursday (alternate weeks) | The ship log drafted from the release notes, with the metrics query's number (asks across partners this fortnight) | Agents |
| Friday (alternate weeks) | Edit, send. Every reply answered the same day | Founder |
| Daily, 15 min | Three first touches, or the second and third touches due, from the sequence in [design-partners.md](../design-partners.md#outreach-templates). Logged in the pipeline | Founder, research by agents |
| Daily | Sign-ups: anyone who signed up but hasn't installed Slack in three days gets one note; anyone installed but without an ask in ten days gets the nudge. Manual, from the sheet, until there are too many | Founder, list by agents |
| Monthly | List hygiene: bounces removed, unsubscribes honoured the same day, the opt-in copy checked | Agents |
| Once | SPF, DKIM and DMARC on the sending domain checked; the founder's mailbox warmed with real mail before any sequence | macbookpro |

## Rules

- Opt-in only, with an unsubscribe link, on every list mail. India's DPDP
  Act and GDPR both want that; so do we.
- Cold mail goes from the founder's own mailbox (a Google Workspace address,
  warmed), never from the product's transactional domain, so a complaint
  never hurts sign-up emails.
- No sequence tool that sprays. A cold mail is written for one person, with
  one line that proves it.
- Three touches, then stop. The third says so.
- Never a tracking pixel in the ship log; replies are the measure.
- One list tool, kept simple (Buttondown, Loops or Resend Broadcasts; pick
  one, don't migrate).

## The first ten moves

1. SPF, DKIM, DMARC checked; the sending mailbox chosen.
2. The list tool chosen; the list created; the opt-in checkbox on the
   partner page and in the sign-up flow ("Send me the ship log", unticked).
3. The ship log template: the five lines.
4. Ship log #1, week 3, to partners and opted-in sign-ups.
5. The cold sequence's three mails, personalised per the templates.
6. The research line per prospect, by the agents, in the pipeline.
7. The three-day and ten-day notes to sign-ups.
8. The metrics query feeding the number.
9. Week 15: the launch-week note to partners.
10. Pricing on: the billing-contact mails Dodo sends, checked for the words.

## What we count

List size; ship-log replies (not opens); cold reply rate and calls booked;
sign-ups who installed after a note.

## Founder or agent

Agents draft, research, keep the list clean. The founder sends and answers.
