# Calendar: the week, and the sixteen weeks

*2026-10-15. Times are IST. The routine fits one founder with about ninety
minutes a day plus partner calls, and agents doing the drafting. Each
platform's own routine is in [platforms/](platforms/README.md); this page
is where they meet.*

## The day

| When (IST) | What | Who |
|---|---|---|
| 08:30–09:15 | The morning block. One X post. Fifteen minutes of LinkedIn comments on ten ICP posts. Ten minutes on the mentions digest (GitHub issues and discussions, X replies, email). Ten LinkedIn DMs or connection notes from the list | Founder; the digest and DM research are the agents' |
| 10:00–12:00 Tue–Thu | Partner calls, India | Founder |
| 18:00–18:30 | The evening block (08:30 US East). LinkedIn post on post days. Replies on X. Five more DMs. Anything inbound gets an answer today | Founder |
| 19:30–21:30 Tue–Thu | Partner calls, US and EU | Founder |
| Overnight | Drafts: tomorrow's X post options (three), the week's LinkedIn posts, release notes on Thursday, the metrics query and mentions digest on Friday | Agents |

Monday and Friday have no calls. Friday afternoon is the content block.

**The Friday block (14:00–17:00).**
1. Read the agents' release notes; fix the words; publish the release.
2. Record: one demo cut or feature clip (under two minutes), and one short.
   The recording feeds next week's X clip, LinkedIn demo post, YouTube short
   and the changelog's picture.
3. Approve the ship log (fortnightly) and the mentions digest.
4. Pick next week's post themes from [content-bank.md](content-bank.md);
   the agents draft them over the weekend.
5. Partner digest to the board; file the issues.

## The sixteen weeks

Week 1 starts Monday 2026-10-19. A week's line says what must be true by
its Friday. "Launch" days are Tuesdays and Wednesdays; nothing launches in
the two holiday weeks.

## Rules of the calendar

- Nothing is scheduled for a day with a partner call before 10:00 or after
  21:30 IST. Calls are Tuesday to Thursday only.
- A launch day (HN, Product Hunt) has no calls and no other posts.
- A week that misses its checkpoint moves the next launch, never the
  routine. The routine is the part that compounds.
- The agents never post. They draft, measure, digest and file. The founder
  posts, replies and calls.
